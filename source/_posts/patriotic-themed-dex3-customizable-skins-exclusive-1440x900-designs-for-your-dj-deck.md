## Patriotic Themed DEX3 Customizable Skins: Exclusive 1440X900 Designs for Your DJ Deck

[![](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/skinpack-coverimage.jpg?resize=573%2C270&ssl=1)](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/skinpack-coverimage.jpg?fit=573%2C339&ssl=1 "skinpack-coverimage")

Our **[DEX 3 DJ software’s](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)** newly revised graphics engine supports user created skins. You can build them yourself using our freshly updated “[**PCDJ** **Skin Designe**r](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)” – this means you can select a different skin in DEX 3’s options to change the entire look and feel of our **_#MixEverything_** DJ software. 

[![](https://i2.wp.com/www.pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/1440x900skinexample-300x186.jpg?resize=300%2C186&ssl=1 "1440x900skinexample")](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/1440x900skinexample.jpg?fit=1030%2C640&ssl=1)

Emiliano Vargas (_DJ Vargas_) of Sweden (_born in Uruguay, and as he proudly pointed out – the first country to win the World Cup_) brought us the [Denon DN-HC4500 skin](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) last week. This week he brings us a **Patriotic Pack** of 1440×900 resolution skins based on the DEX 2 default 2-Deck skin. If you take pride in your Country and you’re from the USA, Sweden or Uruguay there’s a patriotic skin included for you. Each country has 2 versions of the 2 deck skin, with or without a video preview section. The skins included also feature stacked waveform’s across the top, which come in handy while beat mixing for visual reference. 

[![](https://i2.wp.com/www.pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/swedenskin-300x186.jpg?resize=300%2C186&ssl=1 "swedenskin")](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/swedenskin.jpg?fit=1030%2C641&ssl=1)

Skins are always free for DEX 3 customers, and we invite everyone with a keen sense of design to try their hand at creating and submitting their own DEX 3 skin for all users to download. Check out the PCDJ Skin Designer [HERE](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/).

Download the skin below and be sure to let Emiliano know what you think of his DEX 3 Skins by [visiting his website and signing his guestbook](http://www.partydude.se)!
