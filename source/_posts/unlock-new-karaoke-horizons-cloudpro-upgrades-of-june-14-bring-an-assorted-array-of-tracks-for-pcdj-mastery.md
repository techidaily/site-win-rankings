New tunes are added all the time at no additional charge for subscribers, and since you download the files to your local hard drive you only have to sync with the online server once a month to verify you’re still paying. This means you can use [PCDJ Karaoki](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) with the [Karaoke Cloud Pro](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) service offline, without an internet connection. Recently, the terms of service have been updated so that users can start or stop the service as they deem necessary, so there is no longer a 12 month contract!
